---
title: Post-activity questions
description: Three or four questions after each session, and what the AI notes from the answers.
---

After a synced or imported activity, OpenAthlete can ask the athlete three or
four short questions about the session: how it felt, fatigue, sleep, and any
pain. The questions adapt to the session and to the injuries already logged.

Athletes answer them from the activity. A coach reads the answers on the
athlete's activity.

## What the AI notes

Once every question is answered, the AI reads the answers and the comment and
looks for two things:

- **Pain or injury**: where, how bad (out of 10) and whether it is getting
  worse, stable or better. It goes to the athlete's injury logs (Metrics) and
  to the coach dashboard's alerts.
- **RPE**: the perceived effort, when the answers give one. It fills the
  activity's RPE, which the athlete can still change.

The activity shows what was noted under **Noted by the AI**: the pain it found,
or that the answers mention none. Editing the answers analyses them again, and
the new result replaces the previous one.

## Turning it off

The questions and the analysis need AI: the athlete's own key, their coach's,
or the instance's (see [Self-hosting](/docs/getting-started/self-hosting)).
The athlete, in Settings > Profile, or their coach, in the athlete's settings,
can turn off **AI feedback questions and analysis**: nothing is then sent to
the AI.
