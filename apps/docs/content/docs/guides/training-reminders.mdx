---
title: Training reminders
description: A notification the evening before planned sessions.
---

On the iOS and Android apps, OpenAthlete sends a notification at 7 pm the day
before planned training sessions: the session's name, or how many there are
and their names.

- **Local time**: the app saves your phone's time zone each time it opens, so
  the reminder follows you when you travel.
- **Which sessions**: planned training sessions of the next day, as the
  calendar shows them. Races and notes are left out.
- **Once an evening**: if the server restarts around 7 pm, the reminder still
  goes out later that evening, until 11 pm, and never twice.

Turn it off in **Settings > Profile > Notifications**.

Reminders are push notifications, so they need the mobile app and an instance
that can send them (`FIREBASE_SERVICE_ACCOUNT_JSON`).
